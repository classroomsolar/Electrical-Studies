S-RATIO METHODOLOGY

Calculates ratio of local source generation feeding fault ($S = I_{gen}/I_{tot}$). Reflects machine subtransient impedance decay.

AC & DC DECREMENTS

Combined decaying envelope: total asymmetric fault current includes both DC component decay ($e^{-t/T_a}$) and generator AC component demagnetization.

DERATED INTERRUPTING CAP

$I_{derated} = I_{rated} / MF_{NACD}$. Devices with fast contact parting (2-Cycle) demand higher duty multipliers at high X/R.
